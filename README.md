# Roulette Tracker

A lightweight, single-zero European roulette tracker. Open `index.html` in a browser to use it; no build step or dependencies are required.

The code is split into `app.js` for page interaction and rendering, `roulette.js` for roulette and betting calculations, and `storage.js` for local persistence.

Click a number on the table to record a spin. The board labels and groups the three dozens, marks the roulette column beside each row, and includes dozen/column information in each number's accessible label. The app keeps the full session history in the browser's local storage, shows the latest 25 spins, and updates color, parity, and low/high counts as results are added. The streak probability section tracks consecutive and alternating color, parity, and range patterns, and calculates the probability of each pattern extending by one spin as a complete sequence. For Color, Parity, and Range, zero continues the preceding nonzero outcome's streak. Dozens and columns instead track how many spins each outcome has missed since its last hit, showing alerts only for outcomes whose probability of missing again falls below their threshold; zero counts as a miss for every dozen and column. Separate percentage sliders control Color/Parity/Range alerts and Dozens/Columns alerts, and both settings are saved locally. The editable current-cash balance determines stake sizes. Color, parity, and range use a whole-dollar four-step ladder in the ratio 1:3:9:27, with a $1 minimum first bet. Dozen and column alerts use Fibonacci amounts starting at $0.10, with as many steps as fit within a 25%-of-cash budget. Active recommendations are automatically paper-bet on the next recorded spin; if their combined stakes exceed current cash, they are scaled down to fit. Winning Color/Parity/Range bets add net profit equal to the stake; winning Dozen/Column bets add net profit equal to twice the stake. Lost wagers are counted as losses, and total wagers, gains, losses, and current cash are saved locally. **Undo last** reverses the most recent spin's ledger settlement. **Clear history** clears spin history and gain/loss totals but leaves current cash unchanged. These reminders do not predict or improve roulette outcomes. For example, after five Reds, the probability of six Reds in a row is (18/37)^6, about 1.33%.

The breakdown describes past results only and does not predict future spins.
